📖 Censorship-Resistant: The Superpower of Freedom to Spend!

Censorship-Resistance 🛡️

Censorship-resistant money is like a magic shield—it lets you use your money no matter who tries to stop you! In today’s digital world, some governments or big companies might try to freeze your account or block your cash, but this superpower makes sure your money stays in your control, like a secret power-up in a game.

Censorship-resistant shield

Why Censorship Resistance Matters:

Freedom: You can spend your money whenever and wherever you want, without someone saying "no."

Safety: It protects your cash from being taken away, even in tough times.

Independence: No one can control it, so it’s yours to keep and use!

Examples

Bank Money: The money we use today can be censored, this means that the government can take it out of your bank account if it wants to. This does not happen often, but it is possible. Many people around the world have had their money taken/stolen by their banks or had their transactions blocked, some have even received fines.

Bitcoin: With Bitcoin, no single person or company can just press a button and freeze your account or take your money. It’s spread out across a huge network of computers worldwide, like a team of superheroes guarding your money, keeping it safe and free!

Bitcoin is censorship-resistant
