📖 Scarcity: The Superpower of Being Rare!

Scarce 🔒

Scarcity means money is hard to find or make, which makes it special and worth a lot. The harder an item is to find or make the more scarce it is and therfore the more valuable it is. Imagine if everyone had a million gold coins— they wouldn’t be as valuable as they are today, right? Scarce money keeps its value because it’s not easy to get, turning it into a treasure worth hunting for.

Rare gold nugget

Why Scarcity Matters:

Value: If money is rare, it stays precious and useful for trading.

Trust: People trust scarce money because it won’t lose its worth overnight.

Challenge: The effort to get it makes it feel like a reward, like finding a hidden prize!

🧠 Money should be rare and special, like a golden ticket.

Examples

Sand: Think about using sand as money—yikes! There’s so much sand on beaches and deserts that it wouldn’t be special at all. You could grab handfuls anytime, and it would be worthless because everyone has too much of it. Not a great treasure hunt!

Gold: Now, picture shiny gold—wow! Gold is scarce because it’s tough to mine deep in the earth, and it takes time, tools, and hard work to find it. That’s why it’s been a favorite money for centuries—its rarity makes it valuable, like a golden prize in a treasure chest!

Fun Fact

Did you know pirates loved gold because it was so rare? They’d sail the seas for years just to find a little bit, making it the ultimate treasure!
